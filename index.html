What SOV is

Not a blockchain — a Sovereign Mesh. Self-contained nodes gossip peer-to-peer with no centre, and its one new idea is proof of personhood: one human, one wallet, proven by your own biometrics on your device. Identity, messaging, justice, and governance on one protocol.

One human, one wallet

Your palm is your key — processed on your device, never sent to a server. No bots, no fake accounts, no whales who joined a hundred times. Everyone counts equally.

Your money, your rules

Send SOV in seconds for a tiny, capped, citizen-voted fee that flows to the people running the network — not a bank. Fixed 50,000,000 supply.

Messaging & justice built in

Messages route through standard ports and work behind firewalls; no content ever touches a relay. Disputes are settled by randomly-selected citizen juries, not courts.

Owned by the people who run it

Self-contained nodes, no central operator list, no single switch to flip. Anyone can run one, and earn for keeping the network alive.
