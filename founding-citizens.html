Founding citizens

The first 100 people to join SOV Network (Self-Owned Value) are its founding citizens. A network where one real person holds one account has to start with real people, one at a time. This is where it starts, and the places are counted.

What a founding citizen gets: a place on this page for as long as the network exists, listed by the nickname the network gives you (nobody picks it and it reveals nothing about you); the welcome every citizen in the first 10,000 receives, 1,000 SOV; and a say, from day one, in the rules everyone after you will live under. One vote per person, never one per coin.

How to take a place: get the app on an Android phone and join. Joining is a palm scan and a live face check on your own phone; no photo is ever stored or sent. Then, if you want to be listed, send your network nickname through Ask the developers. Listing is optional and nothing else about you appears here.
